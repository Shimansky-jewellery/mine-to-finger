Frequently asked questions

Where does tanzanite come from?

Tanzanite is mined in only one place: the Merelani Hills in northern Tanzania, in the shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro. It was discovered there in 1967.

Why is tanzanite rare?

All known tanzanite comes from a single small mining area. With no other source anywhere in the world, supply is limited and cannot be replaced.

How was tanzanite formed?

Tanzanite formed around 585 million years ago, when intense heat and pressure from tectonic activity created a rare blue-violet variety of the mineral zoisite.

What gives tanzanite its colour?

Traces of vanadium give tanzanite its colour. The stone is also trichroic, so it can show blue, violet or burgundy depending on the angle you view it from.

How do I care for tanzanite jewellery?

Tanzanite is softer than diamond, so store it separately and avoid hard knocks. Clean it gently with warm, soapy water and a soft brush, and avoid ultrasonic or steam cleaners and sudden changes in temperature.
