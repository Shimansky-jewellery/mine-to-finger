From Mine to Finger – The Journey of a Shimansky Diamond

What if you could follow the journey of your diamond from the very beginning?

At the heart of every Shimansky diamond, is a South African natural treasure, combined with the constant pursuit of perfection, passion, and innovation to maximize the brilliance and potential in every hand-selected diamond to deliver the most beautiful diamond you deserve.

01 · Formation

More than 3 billion years ago

More than 3 billion years ago, diamonds were formed under extreme heat and pressure, each one a natural creation, entirely unique.

In the late 1800s, the first diamonds were found in South Africa's Northern Cape, shaping one of the most important diamond regions in the world.

02 · The source

This is where the Shimansky journey begins

For decades, we have travelled back to Kimberley and South African mining regions to hand select our rough diamonds directly from the source.

All our diamonds are ethically sourced from mines that adhere to the Kimberley Process, ensuring responsible sourcing and full traceability.

Read more

In the early days, Yair Shimansky was fascinated by the power of diamonds. The sparkling gems and their store of value was intriguing and mesmerizing. After few years of buying polished diamonds from local dealers, he was unsatisfied with the quality of the diamonds cut and polished. In 1998 Shimansky decided to travel to the diamond mines in Kimberley, South Africa, and buy rough diamonds directly from the mines. The first batch of diamonds was given to a local polisher who polished the diamonds for weight gain and not to maximize their brilliance. This is when Shimansky decided to enter the world of diamond cutting and polishing and setup the first Shimansky diamond polishing workshop in 1999, unique among jewelers to this day.

03 · The rough

We analyse each rough diamond

Using advanced technology, we analyse each rough diamond to understand its internal structure and determine how it can be cut to achieve its maximum potential.

Read more

The Shimansky Diamond’s journey begins when only the top diamonds are hand-selected directly from the South African mines. The selection process can take up to five days. With specialized equipment such as laser mapping, 3D scanning combined with personal skills and experience, only the finest rough diamonds are selected for their potential to become a Shimansky diamonds, making their way to the Shimansky diamond cutting and polishing workshop situated in Cape Town, South Africa.

Out of all the 4cs of diamond grading, the cut is the most influential factor on the diamond’s light behavior that affects its brilliance, fire, and scintillation.

05 · Cut & polish

A Cut Above

Throughout the entire process, a Shimansky Diamond undergoes more than 27 quality checks. This gives us full control over quality at every step of the process.

With the constant pursuit of perfection in everything we do, diamonds are no exception. With years of experience in diamond cutting and polishing, Shimansky has designed and patented innovative diamond cuts that are superior in their brilliance and light performance compared to regular diamond cuts commonly used.

06 · Craft

Craft Process

Once cut and polished, the diamond is entrusted to expert craftsmen who have dedicated their lives to the art.

Read more

With a culture of love and passion every step of the way, Shimansky masters the art of making jewellery in every detail. With years of experience and constant learning, the art of jewellery making is preserved.

Starting with the raw material, pure gold or platinum is alloyed to the karat of choice awaiting the craft to start. Diamonds are transformed into brilliant creations in the Shimansky diamond workshop by our skilled diamond cutters and polishers.

From shaping the metal, filing, polishing, soldering, drawing wires to diamond and gemstone setting, beautifully designed & crafted jewellery is brought to life.
